# Contributing to 25(B)lend

First off, thank you for considering contributing to 25(B)lend! We welcome developers, legal advisors, designers, educators, language learners, and anyone who wants to help build a future where borrowing a book digitally is easy, free, respectful, secure, and 100% legal.

## How to Get Started
1. **Find an Issue:** Browse our open [Issues](https://github.com/25-B-lend/reader/issues) and look for tags like `good first issue` or `help wanted`.
2. **Comment to Claim:** Leave a comment on the issue you'd like to work on so we can assign it to you and avoid duplicate work.
3. **Start a Discussion:** For broader architectural, legal, or vision ideas, open a thread under [Discussions](https://github.com/25-B-lend/reader/discussions).

## Contribution Workflow
1. Fork the repository.
2. Create a feature branch (`git checkout -b feature/my-feature`).
3. Commit your changes with clear, descriptive messages.
4. Open a Pull Request against the `main` branch.

##  A Note on Our Current Stage
25(B)lend is in its early stages. Every issue and feature is meant to be discussed, challenged, and refined by the community before becoming a fixed goal.

Everyone has a voice here:
* **Refining Issues:** Share your thoughts, technical approaches, or critiques directly in the issue comments.
* **Proposing New Ideas:** If you have an idea that isn't covered yet, feel free to open a new Issue or start a Discussion thread.
